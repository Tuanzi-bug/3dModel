# Phase 03.3 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for resetting the homepage hero around a static, realistic, product-led preview.

---

## Design System

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Tool | none |
| Preset | not applicable |
| Component library | none |
| Icon library | lucide-react |
| Font | Inter |

### Source Baseline

- Keep `docs/design-system.md` as the product baseline for language, spacing logic, and general landing-page structure.
- Keep the current Tailwind utility approach; do not introduce a new component system in this phase.
- Preserve Chinese copy.
- This phase intentionally reduces decorative energy instead of adding new motion, glassmorphism, or visually loud patterns.

---

|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Meta / Small | 12px | 500 | 1.4 |
| Body | 14px | 400 | 1.6 |

| Section Heading | 24px | 600 | 1.3 |
| Display | 36px | 700 | 1.2 |

### Type Rules

- Hero heading should remain concise and product-led; avoid stacked slogans.
- Supporting copy should read like product explanation, not abstract campaign copy.
- Chip text stays compact and technical/product-like, never loud or decorative.
- Keep typography calm and readable; this phase should not chase editorial drama.

---

## Color

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | #F8FAFC | Page background and negative space |
| Secondary (20%) | #FFFFFF | Cards, surfaces, navbar body |
| Preview Base (10%) | #E5E7EB | Neutral preview/studio environment |
| Text | #0F172A | Primary text |
| Muted Text | #475569 | Secondary text |
| Accent (CTA only) | #F97316 | Primary actions only |

### Color Rules

- Keep brand orange reserved for CTA and small action emphasis.
- Do not use high-saturation blues, purples, or glowing gradients in the hero.
- The preview environment should stay neutral, photographic, and restrained.
- Parameter chips should use soft neutral surfaces, not bright badges.

---

## Hero Layout Contract

### Structure

- Single-column hero.
- Navbar on top.
- Headline, supporting copy, and CTA above the preview.
- Large static preview below copy block.
- Parameter chips anchored to the lower-right area of the preview.

### Preview Surface

- The preview should read as a product main image, not an in-browser 3D viewport.
- Recommended aspect ratio: approximately `5:3`.
- Product occupies the central visual field with enough bottom space for grounded shadow and lower-right chips.
- Preview card may retain rounded corners, but its treatment should feel restrained and premium, not dramatic.

### Hero Rules

- Do not overlay headline or buttons on the preview image.
- Do not present drag/zoom instructions in the hero.
- Do not show loading/fallback language in the static-first state.
- Do not rely on visible interaction chrome to sell the product.

---

## Product Preview Contract

### Visual Target

- Realistic product preview / studio render / product-photo feel.
- Neutral backdrop.
- Grounded object with visible contact shadow.
- Believable material differentiation across frame, shelf surfaces, and details.

### Explicit Avoid List

- Cartoonish form language
- Toy-like material read
- Overly saturated palette
- Flat, evenly distributed demo lighting
- Plastic-like unified surface treatment
- Stylized/game-asset-preview energy

### Realism Cues

- Key/fill/rim lighting separation
- Soft but present grounding shadow
- Controlled highlights
- Neutral, low-noise background
- Product-photography-like camera angle

---

## Chip Contract

### Quantity

- 3 to 4 chips maximum.

### Placement

- Lower-right of preview, clear of the product silhouette.

### Tone

- Mixed content:
  - Product-value chips such as modularity or adjustability
  - Specification-like chips such as layer count or frame material

### Visual Rules

- Neutral or lightly translucent surface
- Dark text
- Medium corner radius
- No floating glow, pulse, shimmer, or sticker-like styling

---

## Copywriting Contract

| Element | Preferred Direction |
|---------|---------------------|
| Hero label | 模块化货架设计平台 / equivalent restrained product label |
| Hero headline | Product-led, realistic, trust-building statement |
| Hero body | Explain what the user can preview or confirm before deeper design work |
| Primary CTA | 开始设计 |
| Secondary CTA | 查看示例 |

### Copy Rules

- Speak like a real product page, not a concept ad.
- Avoid overclaiming or piling multiple promises into one sentence.
- Keep feature cards below the hero more concrete and less generic if they are adjusted in this phase.

---

## Screen Focus And Hierarchy

### Homepage

- First focal point: the static product preview.
- Second focal point: the headline and primary CTA.
- Third focal point: the restrained parameter chips.
- The first section below the hero should visually support the hero rather than compete with it.

---

## Responsive Contract

Target checkpoints: `375px`, `768px`, `1024px`, `1440px`

### Rules

- Hero remains single-column at all supported sizes.
- Copy stays above the preview at all breakpoints.
- Preview must not crop into an unusable or visually confusing composition on mobile.
- Chips may wrap or simplify on small screens, but should remain attached to the preview block.
- No horizontal scroll.

---

## Motion And Feedback

- Motion should be minimal in this phase.
- Keep standard hover/focus transitions only for buttons and links.
- Do not use animation as a substitute for product credibility.
- Continue respecting reduced-motion preferences.

---

## Accessibility Contract

- Buttons and links keep visible focus states.
- Hero text maintains strong contrast on light backgrounds.
- Any preview image or image-like presentation should expose meaningful accessible labeling where appropriate.
- Decorative chips must not become the only source of critical product information.
